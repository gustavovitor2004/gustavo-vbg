# Vision

## What This Is

This is the personal portfolio and creator hub of Gustavo Gomes — a full-stack developer and content creator based in Bahia, Brazil. It is not a template. It is not a theme. Every decision, from the typeface weight to the section order, is a deliberate expression of how Gustavo works and what he values.

---

## The North Star

**Make the work speak louder than the website.**

Most developer portfolios compete on visual complexity — gradients, animations, particle systems. This site competes on restraint. The work is the hero. The website is the frame.

---

## Aesthetic Reference

The site draws from a specific cluster of design traditions:

- **Swiss International Typographic Style** — grid discipline, editorial hierarchy, black ink on white paper.
- **Apple product pages (2012–2016)** — contrast through scale, one idea per screen, sparse copy.
- **Linear's marketing site** — engineering precision applied to product communication.
- **Raycast's website** — dark-first, confident type, editorial photography.
- **Kinfolk magazine layouts** — generous whitespace, type-as-image, restraint as luxury.

The result: a dark, warm, editorial product. Not a developer portfolio that looks like every other developer portfolio.

---

## What the Site Must Never Look Like

The previous iteration of this site used:
- Purple and cyan gradient palettes
- Neon glows and neon borders (`neon-border-purple`, `neon-border-cyan`)
- Floating orbs and particle backgrounds
- Centered hero with avatar photo
- Grid cards with rounded corners
- Glass morphism card effects

None of these are permitted in the current design. If a proposed change reintroduces any of these elements, reject it.

---

## The Visitor

The visitor is a potential client, collaborator, or employer. They are likely a non-technical business owner in Brazil looking to establish or improve their web presence, or a developer or designer evaluating Gustavo's taste and technical skill.

They arrive with one question: *"Can this person make something I'm proud of?"*

The site must answer that question in under 10 seconds, before they read a single word of copy.

---

## The Product Metaphor

Think of this site the way a physical architect thinks of their portfolio book: heavy paper, tight binding, clean sans-serif type, precise spacing. Nothing superfluous. Every page earned.

The site should feel like something Gustavo *made*, not something he *configured*. The difference is perceptible even when you can't name it.

---

## Long-Term Vision

The current site is phase one: portfolio. Future phases:
- **Phase 2 — Blog:** Written content about building products, freelancing in Brazil, and frontend craft. Accessible at `/blog`.
- **Phase 3 — Case Studies:** Deep-dive project pages for GridHunter and client work.
- **Phase 4 — Products:** Landing pages for any future SaaS products.

Every phase must fit inside the same visual language established here. The design system in `05-DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` is designed to scale to these futures without contradiction.

---

## Acceptance Criteria for the Vision

A visitor landing on the home page should be able to:
1. Understand that Gustavo is a developer within 3 seconds, without reading body copy.
2. See at least one piece of real work within one scroll.
3. Find a way to contact Gustavo without scrolling to the bottom.
4. Trust the quality of the work from the quality of the site alone.

If any of these fail, the site has failed.
